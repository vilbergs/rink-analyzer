## Principle
The lessons say where a player is from the puck, not from the rink. When the puck moves, these words move with it. Our net is at the bottom. Their C has the puck in our right corner.

## Words
Strong side: the half of the rink the puck is in. The other half is the weak side, or the far side.

Above the puck: further from our net than the puck, so higher on the screen. Below the puck is closer to our net.

Inside: between an opponent and our net. A defender wants to be inside.

A lane: the straight line a pass or a shot would travel. Standing in a lane blocks it.

## Check yourself
The puck moves to our left corner. Which side is the strong side now, and where is your wall?

The left side is the strong side now. Your wall, on the right, is the weak side.

## Cue
When the puck moves: find it, then say which side is strong now.
