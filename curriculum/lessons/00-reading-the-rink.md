## Principle
The lessons use a few words for places on the rink. Here you learn them by tapping them. Our net is at the bottom of the screen. You are the blue RW, and the red C has the puck in our right corner.

## Words
The point: just inside our blue line, near the boards, where their defenders stand.

The slot: the ice in front of our net, between the two circles. The high slot is its top.

The wall: the side of the rink. My wall is the right-hand side.

Hash marks: the short lines beside each circle. On the wall, a winger waits there.

Strong side: the half of the rink the puck is in. The other half is the weak side, or the far side.

Above the puck: further from our net than the puck, so higher on the screen. Below the puck is closer to our net.

A lane: the straight line a pass or a shot would travel.

Inside: between an opponent and our net.

Our zone: the end of the rink with our net in it. The blue line marks its edge. Getting the puck out of it is a breakout.

## Check yourself
The puck is in our right corner. Which side is the strong side? Is their LD above or below the puck?

The right side, where the puck is. Their LD is above the puck: further from our net, up at the point.

## Cue
Every shift: find the puck, find our net, then find which side I am on.
