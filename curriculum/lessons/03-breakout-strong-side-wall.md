## Principle
Be stopped at the hash marks before the D has the puck, and decide before it arrives.

## Cue
Our D picks it up behind the net: stop, back to the wall, shoulder check.

## What wrong looks like
You are late to your wall and still skating toward it at 2.0 s instead of stopped there. The rim hits you moving, you take it on your skates with your back to the play, and you are still turning when their LW gets above you on the wall and their LD steps down. You come up the wall slow, the puck gets out late, and there is no 3-on-2.

## Self-check
At 2.0 s, could you name the forechecker and the pinching D before the puck got to you? At 3.0 s, is their LD committed or holding?
