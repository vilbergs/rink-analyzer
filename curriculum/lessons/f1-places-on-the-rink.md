## Principle
Every lesson uses a few names for places on the rink. Here you find them by tapping them. Our net is at the bottom of the screen. You are the blue RW. Their C, in red, has the puck in our right corner.

## Words
The point: just below our blue line, near the wall, where their defenders wait when they attack.

The slot: the ice in front of our net, between the two circles. The high slot is its top, toward the blue line.

The wall: the side of the rink, the boards. As the RW, your wall is the right-hand side.

Hash marks: the short lines beside each faceoff circle. On the wall, a winger waits there for a pass.

Blue line: the thick line that marks the edge of our zone.

Our zone: the end of the rink with our net in it. Their zone is the other end.

Breakout: getting the puck out of our zone after we win it.

The badges: LD and RD are defenders. LW, C and RW are forwards. G is the goalie.

## Check yourself
Their defenders wait just below our blue line, near the wall, for a pass. What is that place called?

The point. There is one on each side of the rink, and the lessons often ask who is watching it.

## Cue
Every shift: find our net, the slot in front of it, then the points.
