## Principle
The weak-side winger plays the high slot, inside his point man, and moves on the pass, not the carrier.

## Cue
Puck on the far side: find my man, get inside him, stick in the cross-ice lane.

## What wrong looks like
You drift toward the puck side, into the middle of the slot, as if to help, and lose sight of their LD behind your shoulder. On the D-to-D pass he has a free shot from your point, and the step you took toward the puck is the step you now owe back.

## Self-check
At 2.5 s, can you see the puck and their LD at the same time? At 4.0 s, did you leave on the pass or on the arrival?
