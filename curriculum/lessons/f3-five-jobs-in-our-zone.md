## Principle
They want a shot with nobody in the way, often from the point. One of our defenders fights for the puck, the other holds our net, and our C has the slot. Each winger blocks the lane to the point on their side.

## Words
D-to-D: a pass between their two defenders along the blue line, to the point with the more open lane.

## What wrong looks like
Our RW leaves the point to help at the puck. Now three of us are near the corner and nobody is between the puck and their LD. The pass gets there alone, and they shoot through an open lane.

## Check yourself
Their C has the puck in our left corner. Whose job is their RD at the point?

Our LW, the winger on that side. They stand between the puck and their RD, so the pass has to go through them.

## Cue
Puck in our zone: find the one player who is your job.
