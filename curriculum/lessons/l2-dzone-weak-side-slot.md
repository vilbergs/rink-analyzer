## Principle
The puck is in the far corner and their LD is at my point. They want a D-to-D pass and a shot through an open lane. Stay in the high slot, inside them, seeing them and the puck, and step out as the pass leaves.

## Words
D-to-D: a pass between their two defenders along the blue line, here from their RD to their LD.

## What wrong looks like
You drift toward the puck, into the middle of the slot, to help. Their LD slips behind you and out of sight. When the D-to-D pass comes, nobody is in their lane, and every step you took toward the puck is a step you now skate back.

## Check yourself
When the D-to-D pass went to their LD, did you step out as it left, or as it arrived?

As it left. The pass and your skate out to their LD both take about half a second, so you arrive as they catch it.

## Cue
Puck in the far corner: see their LD and the puck, go on the pass.
