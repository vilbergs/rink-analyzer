## Words
Forecheck: their forwards chasing the puck in our zone, to stop us getting it out.

Rim: a hard pass along the boards, around behind our net and up to a winger on the wall.

Outlet: the teammate a player with the puck can pass to when pressure comes.

Zone exit: the moment the puck crosses our blue line on the way out.

## Principle
One of our defenders gets the puck behind our net while their forwards chase. Each winger waits, stopped, at the hash marks on their wall, and our C stays low in the middle. The puck goes up a wall, then over our blue line.

## Check yourself
One of our defenders wins the puck behind our net. Where does each winger go, and why stop there?

To the hash marks on their own wall, stopped, with their back to the boards. Stopped, they can take a rim and look up the ice before the forecheck arrives.

## Cue
We win it behind our net: wingers to the hash marks, puck up a wall.
