## Words
Pinch: their defender leaves the blue line and comes down the wall to keep the puck in our zone.

Middle lane: the middle of the ice on the way out of our zone. The far winger goes there to take a pass.

Rush: attacking with speed right after the breakout, before they are ready.

3-on-2: three of us attacking two of their defenders.

## Principle
When we break out, they try to keep the puck in our zone. Their forwards chase our defenders, one of their defenders pinches down the wall, and a forward covers the middle lane. Get past them, and three of us attack two.

## Check yourself
Name the three ways they try to stop our breakout.

Their forwards chase our defenders behind our net. One of their defenders pinches down the wall. A forward stays in the middle lane, ready to follow our far winger there.

## Cue
We break out: find who chases, who pinches, who covers the middle.
